# Product thesis

Payment Journey evaluates whether payment data remains semantically faithful as it moves between formats, systems, and transformations. The central question is: **Did the payment data survive the journey?**

The product should distinguish syntax, semantic correctness, information preservation, structure, origin, and defensibility under a selected policy regime. An artifact can pass syntax checks while still representing a degraded payment.

A factual lineage engine accounts for every material source element and every material target origin. A separate policy engine evaluates those observations against explicitly selected, versioned rules. Findings explain what happened, where it happened, and which rule or authority informs any judgment.

The first proof is deliberately narrow: debtor and creditor postal addresses across a supported synthetic MT103/pacs.008 pair, with names and account references where useful. Broader domain placeholders preserve extension paths without authorizing extra implementation.

Success means inspectable, reproducible explanations, not a generic quality score. Product value and differentiation remain subject to the [discovery hypotheses](problem-discovery.md).
