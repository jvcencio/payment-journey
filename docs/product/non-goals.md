# MVP non-goals

The first release does not implement:

- FAIM or pain.001 adapters, all ISO 20022 families, or generic MT-to-MX translation.
- All seven initial participant roles in the UI or multi-hop visual journeys.
- Real bank data or AI/LLM address parsing.
- Automatic legal conclusions or full MyStandards replication.
- Payment execution, initiation, sanctions screening, or fraud scoring.
- Full remittance, tax, regulatory-reporting, returns, or cancellations analysis.

The broader model may reserve future domains without implementing them. A new fixture, adapter, role, or rule pack must stay within the frozen MVP unless an explicit later decision changes scope. Payment Journey compares evidence; it is not a payment translator or repair service.
