## UX-H1 — VALIDATED within this review evidence

Payment Journey's domain intelligence and scope discipline are present in the engine and documentation but insufficiently translated into the application's primary user experience.

Convergent themes: conclusions follow technical proof; scenario names lead with taxonomy; audience and Debtor/Creditor-only scope are unclear; primary screens expose architecture vocabulary; correct policy/lineage distinctions need explanation; first use does not show the strongest value.

## Decisions

Lead with wire-address migration work and intended banking teams. Start with known building/suite information placed in Street Name. Present prepared fields as payment information and hide JSON behind a disclosure. Derive the answer from observed events, then show scope, coverage, selected guidance, changes and technical proof. Keep field/authority evidence accessible. Explain outcomes and unknowns, persist evaluation context, and distinguish intentionally absent parties from missing data. No meaningful engine capability is added.

The evidence supports these UX decisions, not demand, adoption, willingness to pay, or practitioner usability success. AI-assisted review does not replace human-practitioner validation. VALIDATED here describes the supplied heuristic finding only.
