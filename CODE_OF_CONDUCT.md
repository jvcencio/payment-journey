# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss evidence and design choices without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory behavior, threats, doxxing, and sharing private information are unacceptable. Respect differing levels of payments and software experience, credit others' work, and accept corrections in good faith.

These expectations apply to project discussions, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Any response should be proportionate and explain the relevant conduct concern without disclosing private reports.

**STATUS: OPEN:** a named conduct contact, private reporting route, and appeal procedure have not been established (O-10). This original bootstrap text is not presented as an adopted external code-of-conduct template. Before opening community channels, resolve those operational responsibilities. Do not post private incident details in public issues.
